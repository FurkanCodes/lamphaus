# Lamphaus App — Third-Party Notices

## Heroicons

Every interface icon is drawn from Heroicons v2.2.0 (https://github.com/tailwindlabs/heroicons),
converted to Compose vectors by `scripts/icons/generate_lamphaus_icons.py`. The
subtitles, 10-second skip, picture-in-picture, rotate, next, and compass glyphs
are drawn by Lamphaus on the same grid.
