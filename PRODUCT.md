# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

android

## Users

Lamphaus is for people who curate their own lawful media providers and want one quiet, dependable place to browse and watch on a personal Android device or a shared television. The primary workflow is finding something, understanding it quickly, and resuming playback without rebuilding context on another device.

## Product Purpose

Lamphaus combines user-added compatible provider catalogs, metadata, streams, subtitles, profiles, and playback progress in one account-backed experience. Success means remote-only TV use feels effortless, mobile setup stays fast, and progress remains trustworthy across devices.

## Positioning

A private, provider-neutral media home that treats the phone and the television as different contexts sharing the same library.

## Brand Personality

Precise, cinematic, and calm. The interface should feel like a pre-dawn observatory: dark enough for viewing, with cold instrument-blue guidance and artwork carrying the emotion.

## Anti-references

Avoid noisy streaming-service imitation, oversized promotional chrome, unexplained icon-only navigation, decorative glass effects, uncontrolled carousels, generic card walls, and any language or code that suggests affiliation with another media platform.

## Design Principles

1. Content leads; chrome recedes.
2. Focus and touch always explain what will happen next.
3. Shared data does not imply a shared layout.
4. Privacy is visible in communal-room moments.
5. Failure remains local, recoverable, and understandable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, 200% mobile font scaling, TalkBack, Switch Access, keyboard, D-pad, reduced-motion, high-contrast, subtitle, and non-color focus support. Every core TV flow must work without touch, and every mobile gesture must have a visible alternative.
