# Lamphaus 1.0.0-beta.24

Switch sources without leaving the player, and a steadier next episode.

- A new Sources button in the player lists every source for what you are watching; pick one to continue from the same moment.
- The next-episode card shows the episode's image again.
- Playing the next episode keeps the TV's matched frame rate and no longer sometimes fails to start.
- Known limits: sources that need another app (such as torrents without a debrid service) are not listed in the player.
