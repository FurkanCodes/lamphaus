# Lamphaus 1.0.0-beta.40

Smoother playback of large 4K files, a second player engine, and many new playback options.

- Playback buffers continuously and recovers from stalls and network hiccups on its own, to stop repeated buffering on large 4K Dolby Vision files.
- Playback → Streaming adds Native memory buffer and Parallel connections for files that still buffer (both off by default).
- Playback engine: choose ExoPlayer or libmpv; Auto plays anime with libmpv for styled subtitles. An optional switch tries the other engine when a stream fails to start.
- Dolby Vision gains Nuvio's modes and switches: Convert to DV8.1, HDR10 base layer, Strip Dolby Vision, Preserve mapping, Convert DV5 to DV8.1, and Strip HDR10+.
- Audio options: tunneled playback, skip silence, downmix to Stereo, Quad, or 5.1, and Force AC-3 for optical receivers.
- Automatic skipping for intros, recaps, outros, and movie end credits.
- Default player can be another app, with subtitles, intro and outro times, and progress sent back to Lamphaus.
- Known limits: the download is larger (about 63 MB) because libmpv ships for every device type. libmpv plays 4K HDR slowly and without HDR on most TV boxes, so keep ExoPlayer for those. Phone playback with libmpv is untested.
