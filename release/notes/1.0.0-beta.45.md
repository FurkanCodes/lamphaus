# Lamphaus 1.0.0-beta.45

Add-ons now reach your other devices within seconds, and Lamphaus asks its servers for less each time it opens.

- Add-ons and artwork keys you add or remove on one device now reach your other phones and TVs within seconds, even while Lamphaus is open on them, without restarting it.
- Opening Lamphaus no longer reloads your add-ons and artwork keys every time; they are fetched only after they change.
- Known limits: devices still on 1.0.0-beta.44 or earlier reload add-ons at every launch and pick up add-on changes from other devices only when Lamphaus reopens, until they update.
