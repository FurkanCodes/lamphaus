# Lamphaus 1.0.0-beta.26

Trailers arrive on phone and TV, and the next episode now starts cleanly with the closest matching source.

- Title pages on phones and tablets have a Trailer button that plays the trailer full screen with sound. Turn it off in Settings → Playback.
- On TV, Spotlight Home can play a muted trailer inside the selected title after a few seconds. It is off by default; turn it on in Settings → Appearance → Trailer previews.
- The next episode, or another source picked in the player, now starts like a fresh playback after the current one closes, so the video starts and the TV's frame rate is matched again.
- The next episode picks the source closest to the one you were watching: the same group, add-on, quality and release when available.
- Known limits: trailers appear only when an add-on lists one for the title, and a trailer can occasionally fail to load; the app briefly shows the title page between episodes.
