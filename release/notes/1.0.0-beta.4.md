# Lamphaus 1.0.0-beta.4

Fixes a startup crash that could occur after updating while Lamphaus checks for new versions.

- Keeps mobile and TV open when automatic update discovery or recovery fails.
- Repairs incompatible saved updater state instead of crashing at launch.
- Shows update-check failures as a recoverable in-app status.
- Known limits: verified on an Android 16 TV emulator; physical phone and TV firmware remain to be tested.
