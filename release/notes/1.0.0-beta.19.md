# Lamphaus 1.0.0-beta.19

A new TV top navigation, rebuilt Discover and Search, drawn profile avatars, and stream badges.

- TV navigation now has Search, Home, Movies, Series, Discover, and Library in one centred row, with a profile switcher on the avatar.
- Discover on TV is one scrolling page: pick Movies or Series and a catalog, filter by genre in place, and browse a poster grid that loads more as you scroll.
- Search on TV opens with popular titles, labels results as Movies and Series, and moves from the keyboard straight into the results.
- Profiles show drawn avatars instead of a letter, and a profile can use your Google account photo; choose one in TV Settings > Profiles.
- Source cards show your add-on's formatting in full, and you can import a Nuvio-compatible badges.json to add image badges (TV: Settings > Appearance; phone: Settings > Add-ons).
- Known limits: stream badges are imported per device and are not synced between phone and TV.
