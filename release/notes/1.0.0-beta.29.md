# Lamphaus 1.0.0-beta.29

Plan your evening: see how each source will play, pick up a series with a recap, find movies that end before bedtime, and keep dialogue clear at night.

- Source lists say when a source plays natively on your device, or what changes, such as Dolby Vision playing as HDR10 or audio decoded instead of sent to your receiver.
- Coming back to a series after three weeks, its page shows Previously: the last episode you finished and its synopsis.
- In the evening, Home shows a row of movies that end before your chosen time, and series show how many episodes and how much time are left in the season.
- New Night listening setting in Playback evens out loud and quiet moments and lifts dialogue.
- On Android TV and Google TV, your unfinished titles and next episodes appear in the home screen's Continue watching row.
- TV Spotlight trailer previews no longer stop working until the setting is switched off and on.
- Each of these features can be turned off in Settings: Appearance on TV, Browsing and Playback on phones and tablets.
- Known limits: how a source plays is predicted from its name, so sources that do not name their format show nothing; Fits tonight needs add-ons that list running times; some TV home screens may not show apps installed outside the Play Store.
