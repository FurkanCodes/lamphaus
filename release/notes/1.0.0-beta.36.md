# Lamphaus 1.0.0-beta.36

New reasons to come back: alerts for new episodes, a home-screen widget, cast pages, and a calmer TV when no one is watching.

- New episodes: opt in on your phone and Lamphaus tells you when a series you follow has a new episode.
- Continue watching widget for your phone's home screen.
- Tap a cast member on a title page to see their other titles (uses your own TMDB key from Settings → Artwork).
- Search remembers your recent searches and can listen with the mic.
- Title pages on your phone take their colors from the artwork, and posters grow smoothly into the title page and back.
- A monthly recap in Library shows how much you watched last month.
- Seek previews: add a Seekr key under Integrations to see the frame you are seeking to.
- Ratings open their source page from the title page.
- TV: an optional ambient view after five idle minutes, and Lamphaus is available as the TV's screensaver.
- Known limits: the monthly recap counts only what you watch from this version on; cast pages need a TMDB key saved to your account.
