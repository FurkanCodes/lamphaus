# Lamphaus 1.0.0-beta.44

Two new TV Home layouts, a layout picker that shows each one in motion, and TV pages that fade in as they open.

- Showcase fills the TV screen with the selected title's backdrop and details above a single row, where the title widens in place.
- Marquee keeps the large featured banner at the top, followed by rows where the selected title widens and its details appear underneath.
- Settings → Appearance → Home layout now opens a picker with a live preview of each of the four layouts.
- Trailer previews, when turned on, play in Spotlight, Showcase, and Marquee.
- TV pages fade in as they open; turn off Settings → Appearance → Page transitions for instant pages.
- The IMDb score in the TV featured banner is readable again.
