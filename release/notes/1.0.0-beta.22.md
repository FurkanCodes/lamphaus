# Lamphaus 1.0.0-beta.22

The TV boot sequence on every launch, and the next episode offered as the credits start.

- The TV boot sequence now plays every time you open Lamphaus, not only the first time; the old loading screen is gone.
- The next-episode card appears when the credits begin instead of at the very end of the episode.
- Known limits: when an episode has more video after its credits, Skip still labels the jump "Skip to post-credits scene", even if it is only a preview.
