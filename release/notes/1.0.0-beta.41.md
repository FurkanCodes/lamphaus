# Lamphaus 1.0.0-beta.41

Stream badges now follow your account to every device.

- Stream badges imported on one device appear on your other signed-in devices, and removing them removes them everywhere. Each device downloads the badge file itself.
- Badges you imported before this update are shared with your account the first time the device syncs.
- Signing out removes the badges from that device; they return when you sign in again.
- Known limits: devices still on an older version keep their badges to themselves until updated.
