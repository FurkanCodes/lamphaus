# Lamphaus 1.0.0-beta.12

Improves HDR playback and makes TV display matching settle cleanly before playback.

- Preserves HDR10, HLG, and Dolby Vision output when the connected display supports it.
- Prevents adaptive renditions and cadence jitter from repeatedly switching the display during playback.
- Uses idempotent frame-rate requests for smoother startup and playback.
- Known limits: HDR signaling and available output modes remain dependent on the connected Android TV and HDMI chain.
