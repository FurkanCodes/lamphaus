# Lamphaus 1.0.0-beta.42

New-episode alerts arrive while the app is closed.

- New episodes alerts on phones now ask Android to let their check run in the background, so alerts no longer wait until you open Lamphaus.
- Settings → Notifications says when battery optimization still holds alerts back, with Allow to fix it.
- After an in-app update, Lamphaus reopens by itself on Android 8 and 9.
- Known limits: Android 10 and later do not let an app reopen itself after updating, so open Lamphaus again after an update there. Some phone makers also stop background checks when the app is swiped away; allow Lamphaus to run in the background in their battery settings.
