# Lamphaus 1.0.0-beta.46

If you allow it, Lamphaus can now report why a playback failed or the app closed, so those problems can be found and fixed.

- Settings → Privacy, now on TV as well as phones, has two switches, both off until you turn them on: Performance metrics sends playbacks that failed, stalled, or stuttered, and Crash reports sends why the app closed.
- Reports go to Sentry, the error-reporting service Lamphaus uses. They never include what you watch, your add-ons, or stream addresses, and are deleted after 30 days.
- Each device also keeps a short record of its last 30 playbacks for troubleshooting; it stays on the device unless a switch above is on.
- Known limits: only problems that happen after a switch is turned on are sent; crashes inside the video engines name the library but not the exact function.
