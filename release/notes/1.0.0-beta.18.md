# Lamphaus 1.0.0-beta.18

A cleaner TV player and an accurate stream Info panel.

- New TV player controls: one row of round icons with no labels, a thick timeline that grows while you seek, and the title with its episode underneath.
- Subtitles and Audio panels on TV share the same dark background.
- The Info panel now shows what the audio output really carries (for example "Dolby Digital passthrough" or "Decoded by FFmpeg · 5.1 PCM"), the video decoder, and the measured frame rate.
- Known limits: rewind and forward buttons are gone from the TV control row; seek with left and right on the timeline or while the controls are hidden.
