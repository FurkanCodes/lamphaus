# Lamphaus 1.0.0-beta.20

Smarter skip and next-episode playback, a clock with the end time, and start-of-playback details.

- Skip intro, recap, and credits now use IntroDB with TheIntroDB as backup, and skipping movie credits lands on a post-credits scene instead of passing it.
- The skip button hides itself after ten seconds and comes back with the controls.
- A new next-episode card on TV and phone prefers a source from the same release, shows "Playing via … in 3s", then continues.
- The TV player shows the time and when the title will end; phones show the end time on the timeline.
- Playback opens with the title's rating, year, and genres, and on TV the matched refresh rate and resolution.
- Known limits: anime-only skip sources (AniSkip, Anime-Skip) and parental-guide warnings are not included yet.
