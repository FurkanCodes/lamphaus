# Lamphaus 1.0.0-beta.14

Makes every main Android TV screen much smoother on lower-powered TV boxes.

- Home, search and browsing redraw far less per frame, so moving between titles and rows stays fluid.
- Title details and episode lists open and scroll smoothly.
- Player controls respond without stutter.
- Settings keeps up when you move quickly through its sections.
- Known limits: a Settings section can still take a moment to appear on slower TV boxes.
