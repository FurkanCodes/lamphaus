# Lamphaus 1.0.0-beta.50

Search is faster and no longer crashes with add-ons that repeat themselves.

- Search no longer closes the app when an add-on lists the same search catalog twice; its results show once. Lists everywhere in the app now cope with add-ons that send the same title or episode more than once.
- Results arrive sooner: search asks your add-ons straight away instead of first reloading each add-on's details, and on phones each add-on's results appear as soon as it answers rather than after the slowest one.
- Search starts at the second letter, as one letter matches nearly everything; with one letter, recent searches and browsing stay on screen.
- Coming back to Search, or pressing Search after typing, keeps the results you had instead of searching again, and catalogs that found nothing no longer leave empty rows.
- Known limits: an add-on's new catalogs can take up to 15 minutes to appear in search unless you use Refresh catalogs in Settings → Add-ons.
