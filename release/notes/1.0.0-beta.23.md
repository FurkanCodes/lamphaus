# Lamphaus 1.0.0-beta.23

A redesigned phone app: cinematic details, a fuller Home, and a new sources screen.

- Details on the phone open on a full-screen backdrop with the title logo, every connected rating, one Play or Resume with the time left, episode cards with stills, cast with photos, and more like this.
- My List on the details page now adds and removes the title.
- Home has a taller featured title, slimmer rows of posters with their names, and wider Continue Watching cards.
- The phone's sources screen shows sources as each add-on answers, with quality tiles and add-on filters that stay in view.
- Search results on the phone are grouped as Movies and Series.
- Known limits: the phone startup profile was not regenerated for the new Home; tablets now use the single-column details layout.
