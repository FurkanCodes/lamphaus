# Lamphaus 1.0.0-beta.21

A new TV boot sequence, a cinematic playback loading screen, and faster Discover.

- The TV opens with a new boot sequence: the Lamphaus lamp switches on and its light opens into Home. It plays while Home loads, and any button skips it.
- Starting a title shows its backdrop and logo with a soft glow and a thin progress light, both tinted from the title's own artwork, then dissolves into the video.
- Discover opens about twice as fast on TV with many add-ons, and Library switches in faster too.
- Posters on TV no longer carry a rating badge; ratings stay on Home's featured title and on details.
- Source lists always start from the first source when you pick an add-on filter.
- Known limits: the boot sequence is TV only; the phone opens straight to content.
