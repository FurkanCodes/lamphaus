# Lamphaus 1.0.0-beta.8

Adds included online subtitle discovery, smoother TV display matching, and broad performance improvements.

- Finds movie and episode subtitles through the included OpenSubtitles Stremio add-on, with language preferences and descriptive track labels.
- Improves TV frame-rate and resolution matching across Media3 and MPV playback paths.
- Keeps large libraries, provider requests, subtitle processing, and playback timing more responsive and resource-efficient.
- Known limits: subtitle availability and language coverage depend on the add-on; TV HDMI mode switching remains device-specific and requires physical-device verification.
