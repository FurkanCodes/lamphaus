# Lamphaus 1.0.0-beta.27

See what's coming next: release countdowns, up-next episodes in Continue Watching, and an option to hide unreleased titles.

- Unaired episodes and unreleased movies and series show when they arrive: today, tomorrow, in a few days, or the date.
- Continue Watching now keeps a series after you finish an episode, showing the next one as Up next, New episode, or its countdown when it airs soon. Remove it from the card menu if you don't want it there.
- Hide unreleased titles from Home and Discover rows in Settings → Appearance on TV or Settings → Browsing on phones and tablets; search still finds them.
- The phone and tablet Trailer button setting has moved to Settings → Browsing.
- Known limits: dates and up-next episodes come from your add-ons, so titles without episode or release dates show none.
