# Lamphaus 1.0.0-beta.43

Changes you make on one device now reach your other devices within seconds, even while Lamphaus is in the background.

- Library, watch progress, profiles, settings, and artwork changes arrive on your other signed-in phones and TVs within seconds, and each device downloads only what changed.
- Lamphaus also catches up when it opens and when your connection returns, so nothing is missed after time offline.
- Continue Watching is ready the moment Home opens instead of appearing a second later.
- The TV opens on a finished Home: the opening sequence waits until your rows are ready, for at most a few seconds.
- Signing in to a different account on a device clears the previous account's data first, so its profiles no longer appear alongside yours.
- Background updates are woken through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging; the wake-up carries no account or viewing data.
- Known limits: devices without Google Play services pick up changes while Lamphaus is open or the next time it opens. Some phone makers' battery savers hold background updates until you open Lamphaus. Devices still on 1.0.0-beta.42 or earlier pick up changes when Lamphaus is reopened, until they update.
