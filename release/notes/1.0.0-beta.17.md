# Lamphaus 1.0.0-beta.17

A much sturdier player: smoother streaming, surround sound everywhere, Dolby Vision fallback, and styled subtitles.

- Streaming no longer cuts out for a moment on high-bitrate episodes: the player keeps a much larger buffer, retries brief network drops on its own, and only shows loading when a pause actually lasts.
- Dolby TrueHD, DTS, and Dolby Digital now play on devices that can't decode them in hardware.
- New Settings → Playback options: Audio output, Downmix (fold surround into stereo for TV speakers), Dolby Vision, and Decoder priority.
- Dolby Vision profile 7 remuxes play in HDR10 when the device can't handle them natively.
- Styled anime subtitles (ASS/SSA) keep their fonts, colors, and positions; Arabic, Hebrew, and Persian subtitles read in the right direction.
- Holding left or right on the remote seeks faster the longer you hold, and quick taps add up into one jump.
- On TV, pausing for a few seconds shows the title and synopsis; the Info panel now shows live stream details.
- Clearer audio and subtitle track names, such as "English · Dolby TrueHD Atmos · 7.1".
- Known limits: Dolby Vision profile 7 is shown as HDR10 rather than converted; the app download is larger because it now includes the new audio and subtitle engines.
