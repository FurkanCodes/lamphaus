# Lamphaus 1.0.0-beta.10

Improves playback startup with a cinematic loading state and reliable output matching before video begins.

- Shows artwork, title metadata, and a softly breathing Lamphaus logo while playback prepares.
- Fetches and prepares source tracks before starting playback, including subtitle and audio selections.
- Matches the TV output resolution and frame rate before play and avoids pausing during transient display-mode changes.
- Adds a full-screen subtitle appearance workspace with live styling controls.
- Known limits: devices that reject a requested display mode continue with their current output mode.
