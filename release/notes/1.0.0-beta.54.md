# Lamphaus 1.0.0-beta.54

New icons across the app, a Picture panel in the player, and subtitle timing that moves both ways.

- Every icon in the app, menus, and player is new, drawn heavier on TV so it reads from across the room.
- In the player, More → Picture adjusts brightness, contrast, saturation, and hue for the title you are watching.
- Subtitles → Timing and sync now moves subtitles earlier as well as later on every stream.
- Directors now appear in the Cast row on details pages instead of a separate line under the buttons.
- The phone player has a thicker timeline with intro and credits marks, and subtitle tracks show whether they come from an add-on or the video itself.
- Phone Settings → Playback groups its options under headings you can open and close.
- On TV, the Finished screen no longer loses focus when a video ends with the controls or a menu open.
- Known limits: Picture is not offered for HDR or Dolby Vision video unless it plays on libmpv, and elsewhere the first change briefly reloads the video. Right after moving subtitles later, the line on screen can be missing for a moment.
