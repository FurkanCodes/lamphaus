# Lamphaus 1.0.0-beta.13

Makes browsing on Android TV noticeably smoother.

- Home stops repainting the whole screen while you browse rows; the hero's slow drift now plays only while the hero or top navigation has focus.
- Moving focus between titles does less work: lighter background artwork, simpler fades, and fewer screen updates, with the same look and timing.
- Loading placeholders animate without redrawing the rest of the screen.
- Known limits: measured on the Android TV emulator; frame times on low-end TV hardware may still vary.
