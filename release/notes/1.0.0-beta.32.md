# Lamphaus 1.0.0-beta.32

A new side menu on TV that is one press away from anywhere, Spotlight as the default Home, and a black background option.

- TV navigation now lives in a slim menu on the left that slides open over the page: press Left at the start of a row, or Back, to reach it from however deep you have scrolled, and press Right to return to the exact title you were on.
- Moving through the menu no longer switches pages until you press Select.
- Prefer tabs across the top? Settings → Appearance → Navigation brings the top bar back.
- Spotlight is now the default TV Home layout; if you chose Classic, it stays.
- New Settings → Appearance → Black background turns the TV background and panels pure black, while background artwork keeps its own setting.
- Known limits: on TVs set to crop the edges of the picture, the side menu's icons can be clipped; switching to the top bar avoids this.
