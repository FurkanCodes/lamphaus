# Lamphaus 1.0.0-beta.11

Keeps the cinematic loading surface visible until TV playback is fully ready and prevents late frame-rate switching flashes.

- Keeps artwork, metadata, and the breathing Lamphaus logo visible during startup and TV rebuffering.
- Measures missing source cadence behind the loading surface and matches frame rate and resolution before revealing video.
- Adds regression coverage for TV loading behavior and frame-rate startup matching.
- Known limits: devices that reject a requested display mode continue with their current output mode.
