# Lamphaus 1.0.0-beta.9

Rebuilds the playback controls around an icon-only player, a shared subtitle lift, and touch-first playback extras.

- Streamlines the player chrome: icon-only controls with clear active states, a scrub time bubble, and a compact top bar on mobile.
- Adds lock mode, brightness and volume rails in landscape and wide windows, double-tap seek, drag-to-scrub, and a rotate control.
- Keeps subtitles clear of the controls on both Media3 and MPV, including when embedded ASS styling is preserved.
- Shows the focused control's label on TV while keeping the control row icon-only at rest.
- Known limits: the new rails, gestures, and lock flow have not been verified on physical devices; TV brightness and volume remain with the remote and device settings.
