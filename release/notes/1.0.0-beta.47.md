# Lamphaus 1.0.0-beta.47

Smoother TV browsing and a cleaner hand-off to the next episode.

- On TV Home, a focused card's still and title logo now appear with it instead of a few seconds later.
- Seek previews now show when you press Left or Right on the TV progress bar, and the first frame stays long enough to see.
- The next episode's source is found ahead of time, so Play, Next, and Yes start right away; pressing Next mid-episode shows that a source is being found.
- "Up next" and "Still watching?" now fill the screen in black with the next episode, and after you answer they show it starting.
- The TV player no longer repeats the episode name under the title.
