# Lamphaus 1.0.0-beta.38

Faster starts, working seek previews, and a TV ambient that follows you everywhere.

- Picking a source opens the player at once; add-on subtitles load while the video starts.
- Title pages on your phone open instantly again, with no dark pause before the artwork.
- Seek previews now show frames when you seek, including on the first seek of a title and for episodes.
- Seekr's daily limits are respected across all your devices, and Settings shows when today's previews return.
- Play on a series starts the right episode: the one you were watching, or the next one up.
- On your phone, artwork keys now live under Settings → Integrations, next to Seekr.
- TV: the ambient view works on every screen, and Appearance → Start after sets how long the remote must be idle.
- Known limits: Seekr allows 20 movies and 70 episodes a day per key; previews pause until the daily reset after that.
