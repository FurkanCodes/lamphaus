# Lamphaus 1.0.0-beta.35

Cleaner source switching, steadier subtitle choices, and your language settings applied every time.

- The player's Sources panel now matches the title page: add-on tabs with counts, clearer cards, and the playing source marked.
- Choosing a subtitle or audio track keeps it in view and selected; tracks show where they come from (Built-in or the add-on) and their format.
- New Subtitle language setting and a longer language list; your audio and subtitle choices apply on both players and are remembered per title.
- Up next and Still watching? wait for your answer, and movies and series finales end on a Finished screen with Watch again. A new Playback setting can close the player after a minute instead.
- Known limits: "Original" audio now keeps each title's own soundtrack instead of picking a dub in your device language.
