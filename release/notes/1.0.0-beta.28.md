# Lamphaus 1.0.0-beta.28

Keep a series going: auto-play the next episode, a Still watching? check, and episodes right inside the player.

- When an episode ends, the next one starts after a short countdown. Dismiss the card to stop it, or turn off Auto-play next episode in Settings.
- After three episodes start on their own in a row, the player asks if you are still watching and closes after a minute without an answer.
- Open Episodes from the player's More menu to jump to another episode of the series; unaired episodes show their countdown.
- Known limits: auto-play and the episode list rely on your add-ons providing episode lists and playable sources.
